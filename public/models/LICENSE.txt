Athlete models and animations in this folder
============================================

Made by Quaternius (https://quaternius.com), released under CC0 1.0 (public domain):
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

- athlete-male.glb, athlete-female.glb: "Universal Base Characters" (Standard),
  Superhero_Male / Superhero_Female bodies with the Hair_SimpleParted / Hair_Buns hairstyles.
- athlete-animations.glb: "Universal Animation Library" (Standard), a subset of its clips.

These files are generated by `npm run models` (scripts/build-models.mjs), which merges the
hair onto the bodies, shrinks the textures to WebP and keeps only the clips the game uses.
To regenerate them, download both packs from the author's site and extract them into
the git-ignored asset-packs/ folder:

  asset-packs/universal-base-characters/   (contents of "Universal Base Characters[Standard]")
  asset-packs/universal-animation-library/ (contents of "Animation Library[Standard]")
